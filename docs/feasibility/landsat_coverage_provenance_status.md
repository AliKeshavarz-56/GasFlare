## Preliminary mosaic design

The local inventory has now been audited offline using groups keyed by spacecraft, WRS Path, and acquisition date. The 90 records form 45 groups: 23 Landsat 8 and 22 Landsat 9. All 45 groups contain exactly one stored product for each of rows 205 and 206, with no missing or duplicate expected row; under the expressly limited row-pair definition, 45 are complete, 0 incomplete, and 0 need row-count review. However, all 45 have unequal exact scene-center UTC times between the two members and therefore require temporal/pass review before treating them as one acquisition pass. The maximum within-group time span is 23 seconds. Equal acquisition date is not evidence that these are the same pass. The full private table retains member IDs, timestamps, row counts, missing/duplicate fields and the row-pair status. “Complete” here says only that the two stored row members exist; it does not establish complete A coverage, valid pixels, or an exhaustive search.

For every group, each saved footprint was separately transformed to EPSG:6933, unioned, and intersected with A and all 32 selected county polygons; counties with zero area are retained in the private table. The per-group×county areas and percentages, the union-of-inventory coverage summaries, and full ordered temporal sequences are private. Across the 2025 interval, the greatest successive observation gap is about 16.0001 days for Landsat 8, 16.0001 days for Landsat 9, and 8.0002 days for the combined sensor sequence. Boundary distances from the interval start to the first and last observation to interval end are included in the private report. No expected-pass model was applied, so these elapsed intervals are not evidence of a definitely missing image.

In the saved inventory union, the selected Ilam counties have positive geometric intersection in 2 of 8 counties, covering 2,989.558 km² or 14.891% of their combined area. The selected Khuzestan counties have positive intersection in 14 of 24 counties, covering 30,487.366 km² or 47.604%. These figures describe only the footprint union of the stored query results, not discovery completeness or QA-valid pixel coverage. In particular, the historical nighttime query was Khuzestan-focused and cannot establish comprehensive night discovery for Ilam.
